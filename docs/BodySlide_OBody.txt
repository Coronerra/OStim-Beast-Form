OStim Beast Form 1.1.1: BodySlide and OBody
by That Guy (Coronerra)

BODYSLIDE
- Beast Form ships no body and no BodySlide set. It is a framework: it
  points at the stock vanilla paths, so any body set can be built and
  dropped in. The Riotguard body pack is the first set built on it; it is
  one set among what others can make.
- Build your werewolf and werebear bodies from your body mod's own
  BodySlide projects or, without one, TWO's "TWO Female Body" and
  "TWO Male Body". They build to the stock paths:
  meshes\actors\werewolfbeast\character assets\femalebodywerewolf_0/_1.nif
  and malebodywerewolf_0/_1.nif; werebears:
  meshes\actors\dlc02\werebear\body\werebear.nif and werebear_female.nif.
  Tick "Build Morphs" for OBody.
- Female bodies: two breasts only. Remove the lower pairs.
- Put the BodySlide output mod below Beast Form, Growl, TWO and Monster
  Lipsync in the MO2 left pane.
- Physics: see Docs\OStim Beast Form\Physics\ and the Extension Guide.
- Heads: TWO's BodySlide head builds keep working with the BFA Heads
  plugins (only the expression file changes).

OBODY
- The O menu opens on werewolves, but human presets do nothing on them.
- OBody keeps ONE preset per actor. Picking a werewolf preset on someone
  replaces their human preset, and the human one comes back only if you
  pick it again.
- OBody lists TWO's werewolf presets as female presets.
