=====================================================================
0. THE FRAMEWORK, AND AN INVITATION
=====================================================================
OStim Beast Form is an enabling framework. It ships no bodies and no sex
animations. The Riotguard body pack is the first body set built on it, not
the only one and not a requirement. Anyone can make bodies, animations,
patches and hubs for it; this guide is the contract.

What a body author gets:
- stock vanilla paths that every plugin of this mod already points at
- ModEvents to listen to and to send, and JSON files to edit
- a path contract for BodySlide output
- a breast-physics contract and a reference configuration
- no DLL, no private folder layout, no required skeleton from this mod

=====================================================================
1. WHAT BEAST FORM SHIPS AND DOES NOT SHIP
=====================================================================
Ships: plugins (armor addons at stock paths, race and head records for the
Vanilla core), scripts, MCM, scene files for its own start poses and
couples, icons, JSON defaults, docs, SkyrimNet files.
Does not ship: body meshes, skeletons, textures of other authors,
collision meshes or physics collision files, animations other than the
few attributed in Lite_animation_attribution.txt. For sex scenes you need
a werewolf / Vampire Lord OStim animation pack (or community-made scenes).

3. MAKING A BODY
=====================================================================
- Build in BodySlide, output to the stock file names above. Keep the .tri
  that matches the nif.
- Female werewolf and werebear bodies: two breasts only (remove the lower
  pairs).
- Aroused swaps are ordinary armor-addon records you can override from
  your own plugin (load after Beast Form):
    NakedTorsoWerewolfBeast        0x0CDD87 (Skyrim.esm record, overridden
                                   by every core; male and female slots)
    OBL_WerewolfAddonAA            0x6000833 (Vanilla core, ESL) /
                                   0x8000833 (TWO and Growl cores)
    OBL_WerebearArousedAA          0x83F (Growl core)
    OBFTWO_WerewolfSheathAA        0x9000801 (Growl + TWO addon)
  Repoint a model path or add slots; do not change FormIDs or editor IDs,
  saves use them. The aroused vampire lord and werebear swaps are applied
  to MALE actors only, by script.
- Bones body authors can rely on come from the TWO 4.1 werewolf skeleton
  (also used by Growl werebears): cock chain WWD 1 to 9, the genital chain
  NPC GenitalsBase / Genitals01 to 06, female NPC L/R Pussy01-02,
  VaginaB / VaginaB1, VaginaDeep, anus NPC LT/RT/LB/RB Anus1-2, breasts
  NPC L/R Breast with Breast01 and the P1 to P3 chains, tongue WWTongue
  Base and WWTongue 2 to 5, and NPC Jaw. Vanilla werebear and the vanilla
  vampire lord skeleton lack some of these; check before you skin.

4. PHYSICS CONTRACT
=====================================================================
A body mesh names its own physics (SMP) file in its extra data; the file
names bones and shapes. So Beast Form alone cannot switch physics on.
- Skin female werewolf and Growl werebear bodies to the breast bones
  above and point your nif at your own physics file.
- Docs\OStim Beast Form\Physics\ has a reference breast-chain
  configuration written for the standard bone names (our own values; copy
  it, do not rely on it as shipped; it sits outside SKSE\Plugins so it is
  inert) and the technique for bones that live inside a body mesh.
- Female vampire lord: the vanilla vampire lord skeleton has no breast
  bones. A body author can embed their own uniquely named physics bones in
  the body mesh and drive them from a physics file; this needs no skeleton
  override.
- CBPC users: same bones; the config is yours.
- Collision (tongue, body) is the body author's choice and is not part of
  Beast Form.
The MCM Body page and the log report whether a beast actor has breast and
genital bones (ModEvent OBF_BodyReport).

5. ACTOR PROPERTIES (gender roles)
=====================================================================
SKSE\Plugins\OStim\actor properties\ holds OBLWerewolfMale/Female,
OBLVampireLordMale/Female (and the Growl werebear files). They map the
creature races to OStim gender roles so couples scenes work. Copy the
pattern for a new race of the same kind.

6. MODEVENTS
=====================================================================
Handler signature for every event: (string eventName, string strArg,
float numArg, Form sender).

We send (only when something happened; never periodic):
  OBF_Ready         strArg "110"            after every load; re-send claims
                                            (hook contract version; 1.1.1
                                            still sends 110)
  OBF_SceneStart    kind                    numArg thread id (player scene)
  OBF_SceneEnd      kind
  OBF_TimerPaused   kind                    numArg hours remaining
  OBF_TimerResumed  kind                    numArg hours remaining
  OBF_Feed          kind                    numArg hours added
  OBF_SizeChanged   size label              numArg multiplier, sender actor
  OBF_BodyReport    kind,flags              flags: female, male,
                                            breastbones, genitalbones
  OBF_StateChanged  "mask"                  numArg claim bitmask
kind is werewolf, werebear, vampirelord or none.

We accept:
  OBF_Claim         strArg duty, numArg 1 claim / 0 release
                    duties: key, timer, load, size, prompt, combat
                    (femalebody is informational)
  OBF_RequestSize   strArg size label (or empty), numArg scale
                    (0 = use the label), sender = the actor
  OBF_RequestStart  strArg up / down / left / right or empty,
                    sender = the target actor
No event can bypass a child check: OBF_RequestStart goes through the
same refusals as the key.

Papyrus listen example:
  Event OnInit()
      RegisterForModEvent("OBF_SceneStart", "OnBeastSceneStart")
      RegisterForModEvent("OBF_Ready", "OnBeastReady")
  EndEvent
  Event OnBeastSceneStart(string eventName, string kind, float tid, Form sender)
      Debug.Trace("beast scene: " + kind)
  EndEvent
  Event OnBeastReady(string eventName, string version, float n, Form sender)
      ; claims are cleared on every load: send yours again here
      int h = ModEvent.Create("OBF_Claim")
      ModEvent.PushString(h, "size")
      ModEvent.PushFloat(h, 1.0)
      ModEvent.PushForm(h, None)
      ModEvent.Send(h)
  EndEvent

7. JSON FILES
=====================================================================
Folder: Data\SKSE\Plugins\StorageUtilData\OStimBeastForm\ (PapyrusUtil
JsonUtil format). Read at load and when needed.
  kinds.json       extra races mapped to one of our three kinds, by
                   plugin name plus decimal FormID (never by name text):
                   stringList werewolf_plugins + intList werewolf_ids,
                   the same for werebear and vampirelord. Cannot add new
                   kinds.
  sizes.json       stringList labels, floatList scales (1 to 12 steps,
                   clamped 0.5 to 2.0), optional stringList nodes (the
                   root bones to scale).
  directions.json  per slot up / down / left / right: "<slot>_actions"
                   (OStim action types, comma separated) and
                   "<slot>_label".
  arousal.json     ints low, mid, high: thresholds for the SkyrimNet
                   arousal band.

=====================================================================
10. WORKED EXAMPLE: THE RIOTGUARD BODY PACK
=====================================================================
The Riotguard body pack is one way to build a body set on the framework:
male and female werewolf and werebear meshes at the stock paths, a small
installer, physics files, BodySlide projects. Treat it as an example, not
the standard. If you publish your own set: say which paths you
overwrite, which skeleton you assume, what physics you carry, how to order
it in MO2, and credit your sources.
