OStim Beast Form 1.0.2: BodySlide and OBody (werewolves only)
by That Guy (Coronerra)

There is no werebear BodySlide anywhere, so this is about werewolves.

BODYSLIDE
- TWO ships its own BodySlide projects ("TWO Female Body", "TWO Male
  Body"). Build them into a separate output mod placed BELOW TWO in MO2's
  left pane.
- Which female body you get depends on the installer:
  * TWO (core B) and Growl + TWO: the "Female werewolf body" page.
    "TWO's own female body (BodySlide works)" uses TWO's
    femalebodywerewolf_0/1.nif, so your "TWO Female Body" build applies.
    "Converted softbody female body" uses this mod's body in
    coronerra\twosoftbody\, which is NOT part of TWO's project: a
    BodySlide build doesn't change it (it keeps TWO's morph names, so
    in-game morphs still work on it).
  * Vanilla (core A) and Growl only: always the converted body.
- 1.0 and 1.0.1 always used the converted body, even without softbody,
  so TWO BodySlide presets seemed to stop working. Fixed in 1.0.2: install
  1.0.2 over it and pick "TWO's own female body". Your existing BodySlide
  output mod works as is (no rebuild needed); keep it below TWO.
- The male body is always TWO's own, so your BodySlide build applies to
  males.
- Heads: TWO's BodySlide head builds keep working with the BFA Heads
  plugins (only the expression file changes, the head meshes are TWO's).

OBODY
- The O menu opens on werewolves, but human presets do nothing on them.
- OBody keeps ONE preset per actor. Picking a werewolf preset on someone
  replaces their human preset, and the human one comes back only if you
  pick it again.
- OBody lists TWO's werewolf presets as female presets.
