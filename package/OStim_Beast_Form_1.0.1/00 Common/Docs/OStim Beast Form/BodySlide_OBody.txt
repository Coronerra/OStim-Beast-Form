OStim Beast Form 1.0: BodySlide and OBody (werewolves only)
by That Guy (Coronerra)

There is no werebear BodySlide anywhere, so this is about werewolves.

BODYSLIDE
- TWO ships its own BodySlide projects ("TWO Female Body", "TWO Male
  Body"). Build them into a separate output mod placed BELOW TWO in MO2's
  left pane.
- This mod's converted female werewolf body is not part of TWO's project,
  so a BodySlide build doesn't change it. It keeps TWO's morph names, so
  in-game morphs still work on it.
- The male body is TWO's own, so your BodySlide build applies to males.

OBODY
- The O menu opens on werewolves, but human presets do nothing on them.
- OBody keeps ONE preset per actor. Picking a werewolf preset on someone
  replaces their human preset, and the human one comes back only if you
  pick it again.
- OBody lists TWO's werewolf presets as female presets.
